# Historical competition documents

The project owner explicitly authorized public copies of the final competition paper and award certificate. These two PDFs are preserved byte for byte; their hashes and sizes appear in [manifest.csv](manifest.csv). This is a narrowly reviewed publication exception, not permission to redistribute the remaining private archive.

- [Competition paper](competition-paper.pdf): the original 25-page submitted-work document. It describes historical claims/settings and must not be confused with the newly reconstructed implementation or its disclosed assumptions.
- [Award certificate](award-certificate.pdf): the original one-page certificate, including its historical participant/advisor/institution names and award signatures. These authorized contents and document metadata are retained in the copy.

The software MIT license does **not** apply to these PDFs, their historical text, certificate design, photographs, contest marks, signatures or other third-party material. Their inclusion supplies historical context and does not grant additional redistribution rights or validate the paper's scientific claims. Refer to the respective authors/issuers for reuse permissions.

The public verification script accepts only these exact paths and pinned hashes. Other historical binaries, contest statements, datasets, reference papers and the private archive remain excluded. The runnable MATLAB reconstruction does not require either PDF.
